Exosuit Terminal Symbols is a fixed-pitch subset of Noto Sans Symbols2,
version 2.003, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).
Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/symbols
Source font SHA-256: 876598db5f1e3bef2bc77c4d94c5b0d5d2c1620be4878a06f18fb5434e331688

The subset covers all 256 Braille patterns (U+2800..U+28FF), used by
terminal splash screens, spinners, and plotting tools.
Its glyphs use a 0.6em advance and the browser terminal font's line metrics.
The primary text font continues to supply ordinary characters.

To update from an upstream NotoSansSymbols2-Regular.ttf, install fontTools:
  python3 scripts/generate-terminal-symbol-font.py --source /path/to/NotoSansSymbols2-Regular.ttf
To regenerate the embedded Haxe source from the checked-in subset:
  python3 scripts/generate-terminal-symbol-font.py
To check that the embedded source matches the font:
  python3 scripts/generate-terminal-symbol-font.py --check
